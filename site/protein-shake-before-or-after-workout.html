Protein Shake Before or After a Workout? What Actually Matters

By the Animal Paradise coaching team · Vineland, NJ

Short answer: For most people, total daily protein matters far more than whether a shake comes before or after training. Having one within a couple of hours of your workout is convenient and works well, especially if your last meal was a while ago.

The short answer

Before, after or both can all work. Pick the timing that helps you hit your daily protein target and fits your schedule.

When after makes more sense

  • You train fasted or early in the morning
  • Your last meal was more than a few hours ago
  • You head straight to work or home and will not eat for a while

When before makes more sense

  • You train after a long gap without food and feel weak
  • A shake sits better than a full meal before lifting

How much protein per day

A common range for people who lift is roughly 0.7 to 1 gram per pound of body weight per day, spread over several meals. If you have kidney issues or a medical condition, ask your doctor what is right for you.

What to put in a shake

  • One scoop of whey or plant protein
  • Milk or water depending on calories
  • Fruit, oats or peanut butter if you need more energy

Is it better to drink protein before or after a workout?

Either works. Total daily protein matters more than timing for most people.
