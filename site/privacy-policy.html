This Privacy Policy (“Policy”) explains how we collect, use, and disclose information about you when you use the Animal Paradise Gyms website at animalparadisegyms.com (the “Site”), our mobile applications, or certain services at our gyms (the “Services”). By using the Site or Services, you agree to this Policy.

Animal Paradise Gyms takes privacy seriously. This Policy explains our practices and the choices you can make about how your information is handled.

Terms of Use

Our Terms and Conditions of Use also govern your use of the Site and Services.

Privacy Policy Updates

We may update this Policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we will provide a notice on our homepage or via email.

We do not knowingly sell or share information of individuals under 16 years of age.

How We Use Information

We use personal information to:

  • Provide and manage memberships and services
  • Communicate about promotions and offers
  • Respond to customer service inquiries
  • Improve services and develop new ones
  • Personalize your experience and marketing
  • Detect fraud and enhance security
  • Comply with legal obligations
  • Facilitate business transactions (e.g., mergers, acquisitions)

Your Rights (California, U.S. States, EU/UK)

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to:

  • Know/Access: Request the personal information we have collected about you
  • Delete: Ask us to delete your personal information (with exceptions)
  • Correct: Fix inaccurate personal information
  • Portability: Obtain a copy of your data in a portable format
  • Opt Out: Stop the sale/sharing of personal info and opt out of targeted advertising
  • Limit Use of Sensitive Info: Restrict use of sensitive personal data
  • Appeal: Request review if we deny a privacy request (certain states)

To exercise your rights, email info@animalparadisegyms.com, call 856-839-4536, or use the “Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information” link on our Site.

We will not discriminate against you for exercising your rights.

Data Retention

We retain your information only as long as necessary to provide services, for legal reasons, and for business purposes (e.g., taxes, safety, disputes).

Security

We use reasonable safeguards (technical, physical, and administrative) to protect your data. However, no system is 100% secure.

Children

Our Site and Services are intended for individuals 18+. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

International Users

If you use our Site outside the U.S., your data will be transferred to the U.S.
