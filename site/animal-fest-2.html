The recap

The year Animal Fest got bigger.

On October 25, 2025, Animal Paradise opened its doors for the second Animal Fest. Members, competitors, vendors and guest athletes filled the floor, and the day ended with medals handed out on stage.

  • Supplement and brand vendors on the gym floor
  • Guest athletes meeting the crowd
  • Medals presented on stage, including bikini and physique competitors
  • Big-screen presentations for the whole room
