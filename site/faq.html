Hours and location

What are Animal Paradise Gyms' hours?

Animal Paradise is open every day of the week from 4AM to 12AM (midnight), including weekends.

Where is Animal Paradise Gyms?

Inside Cumberland Mall at 3849 S Delsea Dr, Suite E000, Vineland, NJ 08360. Members from Millville, Bridgeton and the rest of Cumberland County train here.

Is there parking?

Yes. Parking at Cumberland Mall is free, and it is right outside the gym door.

Memberships and pricing

Is personal training included in my membership?

No. Memberships cover gym access and the amenities listed in your plan. Personal training is a separate service you book with a coach. Your first consultation is free, so you can learn the options and pricing before you decide.

How much is a gym membership at Animal Paradise in Vineland?

Memberships start at $60 a month on the 12-month Gold plan. The no-contract Silver plan is $70 a month. A day pass is $20 and a one-week pass is $40.

Do I have to sign a contract?

No. Silver is month to month with no contract. Gold, Premium, Platinum and Diamond are 12-month agreements, which is why they cost the same or less per month with more included.

Can I try the gym before I join?

Yes. Book a free tour to see every area first, or buy a $20 day pass or a $40 one-week pass to train before you commit.

Can I join online?

Yes. Every plan has a Join button that takes you to secure online sign-up. You can also join at the front desk.

Is there childcare at the gym?

Yes. Daycare is a $9.99 a month add-on, with one hour on Gold and two hours on Premium, Platinum and Diamond.

Training and coaching

Is Animal Paradise good for beginners?

Yes. Book a free tour and staff will walk you through every area and show you how the equipment works. If you want more guidance, personal training is available as a separate service.

Do you offer personal training in Vineland?

Yes. Our coaches offer one-on-one sessions, small groups, meal plans and athlete development. Personal training is a separate service from your membership, and your first consultation is free.

Do you offer NPC contest prep?

Yes. Chuck and Lauren coach competitors through prep, posing and peak week, and members get access to a dedicated posing room.

Amenities

Does Animal Paradise have a women's-only area?

Yes. The Lioness Den is a women's-only training space with its own free weights, machines and cables, included with Platinum and Diamond memberships.

What is the Hibernation Station?

Our recovery room, with massage chairs and compression sleeves to help you recover between sessions. It is included with Premium and Diamond.

Does the gym have a sauna?

Yes. Animal Paradise has sauna rooms, and access is included with every membership.

Does Animal Paradise have red light therapy or tanning?

Yes. Animal Paradise Gyms in Vineland has red light therapy and tanning beds on site. Ask the front desk about access and pricing.

What equipment brands do you have?

Arsenal, Atlantis, Rogue, Booty Builder and StarTrac, plus an 80-foot functional training turf and full free-weight floors.
