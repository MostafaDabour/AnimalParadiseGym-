Our story

A gym in a mall. Nobody expected this.

Pete and Andrew set out to bring competition-grade equipment to Vineland. They put it inside Cumberland Mall so it would be easy to get to, with free parking at the door and hours that fit real schedules: 4AM to midnight, every day.

Then they added the things members kept asking for: a recovery room, a women's-only training space, a cafe, a supplement store, daycare and coaches who still compete.

One visiting member put it simply: he can't find equipment like this even in Boston.
