Sauna, Massage Chairs and Compression: How to Use Gym Recovery Tools

By the Animal Paradise coaching team · Vineland, NJ

Short answer: Sauna, massage chairs and compression boots are best used after training or on rest days to help you relax and feel less stiff. Keep sauna sessions to about 10 to 20 minutes, hydrate before and after, and treat recovery tools as a complement to sleep and nutrition, not a replacement.

Sauna after a workout

Many lifters use the sauna to unwind after training. Most people do well with sessions of about 10 to 20 minutes. Drink water before and after, leave if you feel dizzy, and skip it if you are already dehydrated. If you are pregnant or have a heart condition or blood pressure concerns, check with your doctor first.

Massage chairs

A massage chair session after training can help you relax and loosen tight muscles in the back, legs and shoulders. Start on a lower intensity setting and work up.

Compression therapy

Compression boots apply rhythmic pressure to the legs. Athletes use them after leg days and long sessions because many find their legs feel fresher. Sessions of 20 to 30 minutes are common.

A simple recovery routine

  1. Finish training and have a protein shake or meal
  2. 15 to 20 minutes in the massage chair or compression boots
  3. 10 to 15 minutes in the sauna, with water
  4. Go home and prioritize sleep

The basics still matter most

Sleep, enough protein and sensible training volume do most of the recovery work. The tools make that recovery more comfortable and easier to stick to.
