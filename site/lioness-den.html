Questions

About the Den

Does Animal Paradise have a women's-only area?

Yes. The Lioness Den is a women's-only training space with its own free weights, machines and cables, included with Platinum and Diamond memberships.

How do I get into the Lioness Den?

Entry is code-locked. Platinum and Diamond members scan their QR code at the door to get in, so the space stays women only.

Which memberships include the Lioness Den?

Platinum ($70 a month) and Diamond ($80 a month), both on 12-month agreements. Diamond also includes the Hibernation Station recovery room.

When is the Lioness Den open?

The same hours as the main gym: every day from 4AM to 12AM.

Can women use the main floor too?

Yes. Platinum and Diamond members have full access to the main floor and every amenity, plus the Lioness Den.
