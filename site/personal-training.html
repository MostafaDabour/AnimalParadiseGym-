Is personal training included in my membership?

No. Memberships cover gym access and the amenities listed in your plan. Personal training is a separate service you book with a coach. Your first consultation is free, so you can learn the options and pricing before you decide.

Is Animal Paradise good for beginners?

Yes. Book a free tour and staff will walk you through every area and show you how the equipment works. If you want more guidance, personal training is available as a separate service.

Do you offer personal training in Vineland?

Yes. Our coaches offer one-on-one sessions, small groups, meal plans and athlete development. Personal training is a separate service from your membership, and your first consultation is free.

Do you offer NPC contest prep?

Yes. Chuck and Lauren coach competitors through prep, posing and peak week, and members get access to a dedicated posing room.
