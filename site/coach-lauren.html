About Lauren

Meet Lauren.

Lauren competes in the NPC Bikini division, so she knows what it takes to build a stage-ready physique and how much consistency matters along the way.

She coaches women who are new to lifting, women who want to build strength and shape, and competitors preparing for their first bikini show.
