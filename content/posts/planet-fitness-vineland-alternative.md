## Where Planet Fitness wins

**Price.** A Classic membership starting around $15 a month is the cheapest way to get access to cardio machines and basic strength equipment.

**Travel.** With a Black Card you can train at any Planet Fitness, which is handy if you travel a lot.

**A low-key first step.** If you have never lifted and just want treadmills and machines while you build a habit, a budget gym can work.

## Where Animal Paradise wins

**Serious strength equipment.** We built the floor for people who lift. Heavy dumbbells, squat racks, plate-loaded machines, cable stations and an 80-foot turf mean you are rarely waiting on a single rack.

**Recovery is included.** Every plan comes with the sauna rooms. Premium and Diamond add the Hibernation Station with massage chairs and compression therapy. Red light therapy and tanning are on site too.

**Childcare.** Parents can add daycare for $9.99 a month, so you can train without finding a sitter.

**A women-only space.** The Lioness Den is a code-locked training area for women on the Platinum and Diamond plans.

**Coaching that goes all the way to the stage.** Coach Chuck preps NPC competitors, Hakim is a NASM certified trainer, and Lauren is a female coach. Personal training is a separate service, and the first consultation is free.

**Fuel on site.** Animal Cafe serves shakes, smoothies, acai bowls and coffee, and Animal Supps stocks over 100 brands. Both keep the same hours as the gym.

The gap between a budget gym and Animal Paradise is real, so look at what is inside each plan. If you would pay separately for a sauna session, childcare or recovery tools, or if you have ever left a crowded gym because the only squat rack was taken, the higher membership often works out to better value per visit.

## Which gym should you choose?

Choose **Planet Fitness** if your top priority is the lowest monthly price, you mainly use cardio machines, or you need access to many locations.

Choose **Animal Paradise** if you lift heavy or want to, you want a sauna and recovery tools included, you need childcare, you want a women-only area, or you are thinking about competing.

The easiest way to know is to walk the floor. Book a free tour and see it for yourself.
